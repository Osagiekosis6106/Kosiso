# MAMA'S TEST — 120-SECOND CUT
### Dialogue-only shooting script (condensed version)

Setting: modern-day Igbo-speaking Nigeria — a village, then a nearby city.

---

## CHARACTER BIBLE

**ZIKORA** — 18 at the story's start, ageing to her mid-50s by the end. Slender as a teen, softening with age. Deep brown skin. Black coils in a low bun as a teen; greying locs under a headwrap in later years. Simple cotton wrapper as a teen; a plain maid's uniform during the test; a modest wrapper set at the wedding. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: soft-spoken but firm, slips into proverbs when serious.

**EMEKA** — early 20s. Lean build, dark brown skin, short black afro, plain shirt and trousers. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: talks fast when cornered, voice drops low and evasive.

**PAPA OKAFOR** — Zikora's father, mid-50s. Stocky, broad-shouldered, dark brown skin, greying cropped hair, plain buba and trousers. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: blunt, short commands.

**CHINEDU** — late 20s-30s as the story's main adult version. Tall, fit, businesslike posture, deep brown skin, short neat hair in a low fade, sharp tailored shirts. Nigerian (Igbo) accent, polished. Voice signature: measured and confident, softens around his mother.

**CYNTHIA** — late 20s. Slim, statuesque, light-medium brown skin, polished makeup, sleek long weave, designer dress. Nigerian (Lagos, "big-girl") accent. Voice signature: honeyed around Chinedu, cutting around everyone else.

**BLESSING** — mid-20s. Petite, dark brown skin, simple natural low cut, modest blouse and skirt. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: soft, sincere, never raises her voice.

**PASTOR OKOYE** — 60s, slim, dark brown skin, short white hair, clerical collar and robe. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: measured, ceremonial cadence.

---

## SCENE 1 - EPISODE 1 - VILLAGE PATH, DAY
[VISUAL: A dirt path by a stream. ZIKORA — 18, slender, deep brown skin, black coils in a low bun, cotton wrapper and blouse, Nigerian (Igbo) accent — faces EMEKA — early 20s, lean, dark brown skin, short afro, plain shirt and trousers, Nigerian (Igbo) accent.]
[CAMERA: static close-up]
[CONTINUITY: opening scene; no prior episode]

ZIKORA: (nervous, Igbo accent) Emeka, I am carrying your child.

[ACTION: Emeka steps back, shaking his head]

EMEKA: (cold, Igbo accent) I want nothing to do with you or that baby.

[ACTION: He walks away fast]
[SOUND: his footsteps crunching away on the dirt path]

---

## SCENE 2 - EPISODE 1 - THE FAMILY COMPOUND GATE, NIGHT
[VISUAL: A lantern-lit gate. ZIKORA, same description, faces PAPA OKAFOR — mid-50s, stocky, dark brown skin, greying cropped hair, plain buba and trousers, Nigerian (Igbo) accent.]
[CAMERA: static medium]
[CONTINUITY: new location; days have passed since the previous scene]

PAPA OKAFOR: (furious, Igbo accent) You have brought shame to this family. Leave this house.

[ACTION: Zikora picks up a small bundle and walks through the gate]
[SOUND: the wooden gate creaking shut behind her]

ZIKORA: (resolved, quiet, Igbo accent) I will raise this child alone.

---

## SCENE 3 - EPISODE 1 - EVERGREEN TECH OFFICE, DAY
[VISUAL: A bright modern office. CHINEDU — now late 20s, tall, fit, deep brown skin, short neat fade, tailored shirt, Nigerian (Igbo) accent, polished — stands beside ZIKORA, same description, now in her late 40s, softer build, long braids under a headscarf, modest house-dress.]
[CAMERA: slow pull-back]
[CONTINUITY: new location; years have passed since the previous scene]

CHINEDU: (proud, Igbo accent) Mama, we did it. This is Evergreen Tech.

ZIKORA: (tearful, proud, Igbo accent) You did not waste my tears.

[ACTION: She claps her hands together once]
[SOUND: her hands clapping]

---

## SCENE 4 - EPISODE 1 - THE MANSION LIVING ROOM, NIGHT
[VISUAL: A grand living room. CHINEDU, same description, holds a glass. ZIKORA, same description, now in a plain maid's uniform, rushes in. CYNTHIA — late 20s, slim, light-medium brown skin, sleek weave, designer dress, Nigerian (Lagos) accent — stands frozen nearby.]
[CAMERA: handheld follow]
[CONTINUITY: new location; weeks have passed since the previous scene]

ZIKORA: (urgent, Igbo accent) Chinedu, wait. You can't drink that.

CYNTHIA: (panicked, Lagos accent) This old woman is crazy, Chinedu.

ZIKORA: (revealing, Igbo accent) Cynthia, this is Madam Zikora, my mother. This was all a test. I know what you put in that glass.

CHINEDU: (cold, Igbo accent) Pack your things and leave, Cynthia.

[ACTION: Cynthia is led to the door]
[SOUND: the front door closing firmly]

---

## SCENE 5 - EPISODE 1 - CHINEDU'S SMALL APARTMENT, DAY
[VISUAL: A bare apartment with a few boxes. CHINEDU, same description, sits at a small table. BLESSING — mid-20s, petite, dark brown skin, simple low cut, modest blouse and skirt, Nigerian (Igbo) accent — sets down a bag of food.]
[CAMERA: static medium]
[CONTINUITY: new location; weeks have passed since the previous scene]
[SOUND: the food bag crinkling as it's set down]

CHINEDU: (testing her, quiet, Igbo accent) The company collapsed. I have nothing now.

BLESSING: (warm, Igbo accent) You are not your money, Chinedu. I brought you food.

[ACTION: Chinedu looks at her, moved]

CHINEDU: (to himself, Igbo accent) Everyone else left. Only Blessing stayed.

---

## SCENE 6 - EPISODE 1 - THE MANSION GATE, DAY
[VISUAL: The mansion gate stands open. CHINEDU, same description, and BLESSING, same description, walk up together. ZIKORA, same description, waits in the doorway, back in her own clothes.]
[CAMERA: slow push-in]
[CONTINUITY: new location; days have passed since the previous scene]
[SOUND: the gate creaking open]

CHINEDU: (gentle, Igbo accent) No more lies. This is my mother, Madam Zikora.

BLESSING: (astonished, Igbo accent) I thought you were just a maid.

[ACTION: Chinedu drops to one knee]

CHINEDU: (heartfelt, Igbo accent) Will you do me the honor of being my wife?

BLESSING: (joyful, tearful, Igbo accent) Yes, Chinedu. Yes.

[ACTION: He slides a ring onto her finger; Zikora claps from the doorway]
[SOUND: clapping from the doorway]

---

## SCENE 7 - EPISODE 1 - THE WEDDING VENUE, DAY
[VISUAL: A decorated venue. PASTOR OKOYE — 60s, slim, dark brown skin, short white hair, clerical collar and robe, Nigerian (Igbo) accent — stands before CHINEDU and BLESSING, same descriptions, in wedding attire. ZIKORA, same description, watches from the front row.]
[CAMERA: static medium]
[CONTINUITY: new location; time has passed since the previous scene]

PASTOR OKOYE: (ceremonial, Igbo accent) I now declare you husband and wife.

[ACTION: The couple embraces; guests applaud]
[SOUND: applause across the venue]

ZIKORA: (closing line, quiet, Igbo accent) Love did not come late. It came at the right time.

**[END]**
