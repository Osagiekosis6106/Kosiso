- **"airspeed memory"** → read `airspeed-memory.md` and write all image/video prompts in that style (image-to-video from a real aircraft reference, Airspeed-style 3D studio look).
- **"america memory"**, or any mention of modeling **When America Made Everything** / a lost-American-brands or factory-towns channel → read `america-memory.md` and follow it. For every script, tag each 3–5 s beat as archival film, archival photo, archival ad, modern stock, graphic or AI image/motion; say exactly which free source and search terms to use for the real-footage beats; give AI prompts only for the rest; end with the free-footage shopping list and a filled `america-sourcing-log.md`.
