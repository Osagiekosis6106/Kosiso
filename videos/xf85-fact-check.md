## Claim by claim

| Script line | Verdict | What the sources say |
|---|---|---|
| No landing gear, a hook on top of the nose | ✅ | Retractable hook engaging a trapeze (S1, S3) |

| "probably the smallest jet fighter the US Air Force ever ordered" | ✅ ✏️ | The USAF museum says "the smallest American jet fighter ever built" (S1). You can drop "probably". |
| "barely longer than a car" **and** "shorter than most family cars" | ✏️ | These **contradict each other**. 14 ft 10 in = 4.52 m, about the length of a family car. Use "about the length of a family car" in both places. |

| Early jets could fly only an hour or two | ✅ | General fact; no specific figure is claimed |
