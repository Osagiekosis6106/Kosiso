# MAMA'S TEST
### Dialogue-Only Shooting Script — adapted from the submitted story

Setting: modern-day (with a rural opening) Igbo-speaking Nigeria — a village, then a nearby city.

---

## CHARACTER BIBLE

**ZIKORA** — 18 at the story's start, ageing to her mid-50s by the end. Slender build as a teen, softening with age. Deep brown skin. Black natural coils in a low bun as a teen; long braids under a headscarf as a young mother; greying locs under a headwrap in later years. Simple cotton wrapper-and-blouse as a teen; modest house-dresses and headscarves as a working mother; a plain wrapper set (and a maid's uniform during the "Nnenna" test) in later scenes. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: soft-spoken but firm, short warm sentences, slips into proverbs when serious.

**EMEKA** — early 20s at the start, late 40s on his return. Lean as a youth, heavier and softer in middle age. Dark brown skin. Short black afro as a young man; trimmed hair greying at the temples later. Plain shirt and trousers as a youth; a well-cut shirt and blazer as an older man. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: talks fast when cornered, drops to a low evasive murmur when guilty.

**PAPA OKAFOR** — Zikora's father, mid-50s. Stocky, broad-shouldered. Dark brown skin. Greying hair cropped short. Plain traditional buba and trousers. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: blunt, raises his voice fast, speaks in short commands.

**MAMA OKAFOR** — Zikora's mother, early 50s. Average, soft build. Brown skin. Greying hair under a tied headscarf. Wrapper, blouse, headscarf. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: sharp-tongued, sighs before she speaks, mixes pity with scorn.

**MADAM IJEOMA** — Zikora's first employer, late 30s. Solid, upright build. Brown skin. Hair relaxed and neatly packed. Smart house-dress and headtie. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: crisp, transactional, gives instructions without waiting for a reply.

**MAMA NKECHI** — a market trader, 40s across the story. Sturdy build. Dark brown skin, sun-worn. Headscarf, wrapper, apron over it for market work. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: singsong market patter, quick and warm with regular customers.

**CHINEDU** — newborn to boyhood (~10) to a grown man (~30s). As an adult: tall, fit, upright businesslike posture. Deep brown skin. Short neat hair, low fade as an adult. Plain school uniform as a boy; sharp tailored shirts and suits as a man. Nigerian (Igbo) accent, more polished/corporate as an adult. Voice signature: earnest as a boy; measured and confident as a man, but softens instantly around his mother.

**SUITOR (OKENWA)** — a man who courts young widowed-in-practice Zikora, early 30s. Average build. Brown skin. Short hair. Plain shirt, trousers. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: quiet, wounded, chooses his words carefully.

**BURSAR MR. ADIGWE** — school bursar, 50s. Heavyset. Brown skin. Grey hair, balding. Shirt, tie, worn cardigan. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: formal, clipped, precise about money.

**CYNTHIA** — late 20s. Slim, statuesque. Light-medium brown skin, polished makeup. Sleek long weave. Designer dresses, heels, jewelry. Nigerian (Lagos, "big-girl") English accent. Voice signature: honeyed and charming around Chinedu, clipped and cutting around everyone else.

**JAMES** — early 30s. Average, well-fed build. Brown skin. Short neat hair. Casual designer wear. Nigerian (Lagos) English accent. Voice signature: breezy and warm when things are good, clipped and distant when asked for help.

**THUG** — 20s. Wiry, restless build. Dark brown skin. Close-cropped hair, cap. Worn t-shirt, joggers. Nigerian (Lagos street) accent. Voice signature: harsh, quick, backs down fast once challenged.

**BLESSING** — mid-20s. Petite, average build. Dark brown skin. Simple natural low cut / neat braids. Modest office wear — blouse, skirt, flat shoes. Nigerian (Igbo) accent, gentle. Voice signature: soft, sincere, quick to reassure, never raises her voice.

**NEIGHBOR (MAMA CHIDINMA)** — Zikora's neighbor, 40s. Round build. Brown skin. Headscarf. Housedress. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: chatty, knows everyone's business, talks with her hands.

**ADAEZE (Cynthia's friend, phone voice)** — late 20s, heard only through the phone speaker. Voice signature: fast, gossipy, egging Cynthia on. (Physical description not visible on screen; voice only.) Nigerian (Lagos) English accent.

**PASTOR OKOYE** — wedding officiant, 60s. Slim, upright. Dark brown skin. Short white hair. Clerical collar and robe. Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Voice signature: measured, warm, ceremonial cadence.

## SCENE 1 - EPISODE 1 - VILLAGE PATH BY THE STREAM, LATE AFTERNOON
[VISUAL: A dirt path lined with banana leaves near a stream. ZIKORA — 18, slender, deep brown skin, black coils in a low bun, simple cotton wrapper and blouse, Nigerian (Igbo) accent — stands close to EMEKA — early 20s, lean, dark brown skin, short black afro, plain shirt and trousers, Nigerian (Igbo) accent. Golden late-afternoon light through the leaves.]
[CAMERA: static medium]

EMEKA: (tender, Igbo accent) You are the only girl for me, Zikora. One day everyone will call you my wife.

[ACTION: Zikora smiles and looks down, then back up at him]

ZIKORA: (shy, Igbo accent) Then I will wait for that day, Emeka.

[ACTION: She takes his hand]
[SOUND: leaves rustling as she shifts her weight on the dirt path]

## SCENE 1 - EPISODE 2 - VILLAGE PATH BY THE STREAM, DAY (WEEKS LATER)
[VISUAL: Same path, midday. ZIKORA, same description, now with a hand pressed to her stomach, facing EMEKA, same description, a few paces from her.]
[CAMERA: static close-up]

[ACTION: Zikora clasps her hands together, working up the courage to speak]

ZIKORA: (nervous, Igbo accent) Emeka, I am carrying your child.

[ACTION: Emeka steps back and rubs his face with both hands]

EMEKA: (panicked, Igbo accent) I am not ready to be a father. I cannot do this.

ZIKORA: (pleading, Igbo accent) Emeka, please, we can face this together.

EMEKA: (cold, Igbo accent) I want nothing to do with you or that baby.

[ACTION: He turns and walks away fast]
[SOUND: his footsteps crunching away on the dirt path]

ZIKORA: (breaking, Igbo accent) Emeka, please do not leave me. Emeka!

[ACTION: She runs a few steps after him, then stops, alone]

EMEKA: (from a distance, hard, Igbo accent) Forget me, Zikora. Leave this house before morning.
